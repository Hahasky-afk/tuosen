Quality checks before shipment

  • 01
    MeasurementsKey points checked against your approved sample and size spec.
  • 02
    Seams and stitchingSkipped stitches, open seams and puckering are pulled and fixed.
  • 03
    Print, embroidery and labelsPosition, colour and wash label checked against the sample.
  • 04
    FinishingLoose threads trimmed. Pieces with stains or holes are separated for rework or rejection. Each piece folded and bagged.
  • 05
    Count and cartonsQuantity per size and colour, carton marks and packing list.
  • 06
    Pre-shipment photos & inspectionPhotos and video of your bulk goods before they ship. Independent third-party inspection can be arranged.

Frequently asked questions

Are you a garment factory or a trading company?

We own two garment factories in Jieyang, Guangdong, China, with cutting, sewing, finishing and packing in house. Video factory tours are welcome by appointment.

What is your minimum order quantity (MOQ)?

500 pieces per style. Colours and sizes can be mixed within a style.

Can you produce to US and European size standards?

Yes. We have long produced plus sizes and have proven big-size patterns and bulk experience. We adjust patterns to your size chart or sample, and selected men's styles go up to 8XL.

What OEM / ODM customisation do you offer?

Development from your sample, pattern adjustment, fabric selection, logo printing, embroidery, brand labels and custom packaging.

How long do sampling and bulk production take?

Samples for selected styles from 3 working days. Repeat orders about 7–14 working days. Final lead time is confirmed by quantity, fabric and workmanship.

How do I get a quotation?

Send product photos or a tech pack, order quantity, sizes and customisation needs. We quote once the specifications are confirmed.

How do you ensure product quality?

We produce and inspect against the approved sample and size chart, covering measurements, stitching, print and embroidery, and packing. Pre-shipment photos are available and third-party inspection is supported.

How is international shipping arranged?

We ship FOB Shantou or Shenzhen, work with your nominated forwarder, and can help arrange sea or air freight.
