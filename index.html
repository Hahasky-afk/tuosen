    Seams and stitchingSkipped stitches, open seams and puckering are pulled and fixed.

Straight answer: we do not hold BSCI, WRAP or OEKO-TEX certificates today. If your order needs them, we are not the right factory yet. For everything else, see it for yourself on video or send an inspector.

About us

A Jieyang factory
that sells what it makes

Tuosen Apparel (揭阳拓森服饰) was registered in 2025 and runs two factories in Jieyang, eastern Guangdong. The company is new; our production team is not. They have made garments for Chinese exporters for more than 10 years, including more than 3,000,000 pieces of one milk-silk T-shirt. Now we quote overseas buyers directly, so you deal with the people who cut and sew your order.

We also sell under our own label, Chaoshanku (潮衫酷), from our wholesale stalls. That keeps us close to what actually sells. Labour in Jieyang costs less than in Guangzhou, and we pass that on.
