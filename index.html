A Jieyang factory that sells what it makes

Tuosen Apparel (揭阳拓森服饰) was registered in 2025 and runs two factories in Jieyang, eastern Guangdong. The company is new; our production team is not. They have made garments for Chinese exporters for more than 10 years. Now we quote overseas buyers directly, so you deal with the people who cut and sew your order.

We also sell under our own label, Chaoshanku (潮衫酷), from our wholesale stalls. That keeps us close to what actually sells. Labour in Jieyang costs less than in Guangzhou, and we pass that on.

Frequently asked questions

Are you a factory or a trading company?

A factory. We run two garment factories in Jieyang, Guangdong, and cut, sew, finish and pack your order ourselves. We can show you the line on a live video call any working day.

What is your minimum order?

500 pieces per style. You can mix colours and sizes within that style.

How long do samples and bulk take?

A sample takes about 3 days. Bulk production takes about 7–14 days after you approve the sample, depending on quantity and decoration.

Do you make plus sizes?

Yes. Most of our styles run from S or M up to 8XL, and big sizes are part of our normal production.

Can you put my logo on the garments?

Yes: screen print, heat transfer, embroidery, woven neck labels, care labels, hang tags and custom polybags.

Why are there no prices on the website?

Price depends on fabric, weight, decoration and quantity. Send a photo and quantity and we quote a price per piece, FOB, within 24 hours.

How do I pay?

30% T/T deposit and 70% before shipment, after you have seen photos and video of your bulk goods. Payment goes to our company account only.

Do you have BSCI, WRAP or OEKO-TEX certificates?

No, not today. If your buyer requires them, we are not the right factory yet. You are welcome to send your own inspector.

Where do you ship from?

FOB Shantou or FOB Shenzhen. We can introduce a freight forwarder for sea or air freight to your port.
