About us

A Jieyang factory that sells what it makes

Tuosen Apparel (拓森制衣) was registered in 2025 and runs two factories in Jieyang, in eastern Guangdong. The company is new; our production team is not. They have made garments for Chinese exporters for more than 10 years. Until now most of our output has gone abroad through Chinese export trading companies, including more than 3,000,000 pieces of one milk-silk T-shirt. We now quote overseas buyers directly, so you deal with the people who cut and sew your order.

We also sell under our own label, Chaoshanku (潮衫酷), online and from our wholesale stall. That keeps us close to what actually sells, and it is why we can hold stable prices on large runs of simple styles.

Labour in Jieyang costs less than in Guangzhou, and we pass that on. Our prices are usually lower than Guangzhou factories for the same basics.
