Quality checks before shipment

  • 01
    MeasurementsKey points checked against your approved sample and size spec.
  • 02
    Seams and stitchingSkipped stitches, open seams and puckering are pulled and fixed.
  • 03
    Print, embroidery and labelsPosition, colour and wash label checked against the sample.
  • 04
    FinishingLoose threads trimmed. Pieces with stains or holes are separated for rework or rejection. Each piece folded and bagged.
  • 05
    Count and cartonsQuantity per size and colour, carton marks and packing list.
  • 06
    Pre-shipment photos & inspectionPhotos and video of your bulk goods before they ship. Independent third-party inspection can be arranged.

Frequently asked questions

Are you a factory or a trading company?

A factory. We run two garment factories in Jieyang, Guangdong, and cut, sew, finish and pack your order ourselves. We can show you the line on a live video call any working day.

What is your minimum order?

500 pieces per style. You can mix colours and sizes within that style.

How long do samples and bulk take?

Samples take from 3 working days for selected styles. Bulk production typically takes 7–14 working days for repeat styles, depending on quantity, fabric availability and customisation. We confirm the lead time before you place the order.

Do you make plus sizes?

Yes. Men's sizes go up to 8XL on selected styles, and most of our own styles run from S or M to 8XL. Detailed garment measurements are available for review before production.

Can you put my logo on the garments?

Yes: screen print, heat transfer, embroidery, woven neck labels, care labels, hang tags and custom polybags.

Why are there no prices on the website?

Price depends on fabric, weight, decoration and quantity. Send a photo and quantity and we reply within 24 hours, then confirm a per-piece FOB price once the specifications are clear.

Where do you ship from?

FOB Shantou or FOB Shenzhen. We can introduce a freight forwarder for sea or air freight to your port.
